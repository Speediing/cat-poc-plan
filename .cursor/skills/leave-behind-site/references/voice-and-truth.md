# Voice and truth

## Voice

Write like a sharp colleague’s memo.

- Short declarative sentences. Vary rhythm; avoid symmetric triad lists.
- First person plural for Cursor actions (“we cover token costs”).
- Third person for customer facts (“their DPM org owns backlog…”).
- Sentence-case headings. No decorative emoji. Minimal bold.
- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs over abstract metaphor (“substrate”, “flywheel”, “north star”).

### Banned texture

revolutionary, cutting-edge, transformative, seamless, leverage, unlock synergy,
delve, landscape (abstract), testament, robust solution, “not just X but Y”,
fake precision ROI %, unaudited productivity multipliers.

### Allowed texture

Their words paraphrased without quotation marks unless truly verbatim and necessary.
Pilot terms Cursor actually offers (e.g. 30 days, 100% token cover, &lt;1 day standup).
Numbers they stated (keep their hedging: “about 700”, “close to tens of millions”).

## Truth discipline

1. Cite the canonical meeting at the top of `content.ts` and `CONTENT.md` (title, date, Granola id if any).
2. Every factual claim → that meeting or standard pilot terms. If unsure, cut or label “proposed”.
3. Never fabricate quotes. Paraphrase.
4. Forward-looking items are explicitly proposed/recommended.
5. When a newer meeting supersedes an older one, rewrite — don’t blend contradictory spines.

## Product / tooling line

Default formula:

> Their teams build the products. Cursor accelerates the teams that do.

If a sentence can be read as Cursor owning their AI product work, rewrite it.

## Scannability

- No paragraph longer than ~4 rendered lines
- Parallel ideas → cards, tables, or staged rows — not prose walls
- One callout box maximum per section
- Section skimmable from number + navLabel + heading + bold lead-ins alone

## Copy edit pass

Run an unslop pass before ship: cut puffery, AI vocabulary, em-dash overuse, synonym cycling, chatbot closers (“I hope this helps”).
